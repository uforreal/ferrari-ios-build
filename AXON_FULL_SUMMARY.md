### II. THE ARCHITECTURAL PILLARS (Context)

#### 1. The "Native Language" Concept

Instead of the AI constantly asking a cloud server "What should I say?", we built a **Social Reflex Map**.

- **The Theory:** Like a human baby learning a maternal language, the AI learns the _rhythm_ of your work.
- **The Implementation:** It translates "High Activity" into "Silence/Respect" and "Stillness/Error" into "Curiosity/Hmm" or "Reassurance."

#### 2. The Living Library (The Cortex)

We implemented a tiered knowledge system to ensure the AI only downloads what is necessary.

- **Tier 1 (Permanent):** Professional tools like SolidWorks/VS Code. It downloads full manuals once, digests them, and keeps them forever.
- **Tier 2 (Transient):** Random research (e.g., a movie plot). It fetches a summary and deletes it after 24 hours to prevent "Library Rot."

#### 3. The Digester (Format Translation)

We established that PDFs are for humans, not AI.

- **The Strategy:** When a manual is fetched, a **Digester** converts it into an **AI-Native JSON Map**. This allows the local script to "know" the entire manual instantly without needing to "read" sentences.
